SECTION 2b: Results Discussion

Q1: The current Taiwan results are realistic but not state-of-the-art. The best cost model has ROC-AUC 0.785 and PR-AUC 0.564, but its accuracy is only 0.628 because threshold 0.15 is very aggressive.

Q2: The low F1 is mainly caused by low precision. The model catches many defaults, but it also flags many non-default customers. Therefore, high recall is purchased with too many false positives.

Q3: The results are not suspiciously high. That is good for leakage control, but it also means I should not overstate the model. The current system is better framed as screening/manual-review support than as an automatic lending decision system.

Q4: The expected contribution is not raw accuracy. The defensible contribution is a decision-aware framework that evaluates discrimination, calibration, cost, explanation stability context, ablation, and external validation.
